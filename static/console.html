Getting started

Four steps. Your first fridge is protected once its sensor’s readings are arriving. Each one ticks itself when it is done.

    The %%CHECKLIST_APP%% connection, things to do from a meeting, the full activity log and tools for bigger estates are under Advanced.

    Devices knocking

    Sensors that are sending us readings and are not set up yet. Nothing from them is being watched until you add them — which takes one tap.

    Your fridges and freezers

    Every unit we are watching, and how cold it is right now.

    Reading the estate…

    Problems

    Something went out of its safe range. These are the ones that still need dealing with.

    Heading for trouble

    Still fine, but drifting the wrong way. A chance to fix it before it is a problem.

    Proof of monitoring Owners only

    Show your insurer proof that your fridges are watched 24/7. A one-page report for a location and a month, with a link anyone can use to check it is genuine. Nobody sees it until you share the link, and you can switch the link off.

    Insurance packs

    If food spoils, your insurer wants proof of what happened and when. We hold the readings, so we can assemble it in one click. This is your insurer paying out, not us.

    Your safety records

    Every reading, saved and sealed, so an inspector or an insurer gets a fact instead of a promise.

    Log for an inspector Printable

    Every unit's temperatures for the dates you pick, each time one went out of range, and who answered it. One click to print or save as PDF.

    How you compare

    Your record against other places in the same industry.

    Your locations

    The buildings your units are spread across.

    How we reach you

    Text and phone call, to the people under Who gets called.

    Also send alerts to Slack or Teams (optional)

    Fetch from a sensor cloud

    Some sensors only talk to their own vendor’s cloud and never to us. Point us at that cloud’s address and we fetch from it on a timer.

    %%CHECKLIST_APP%% connection Owners only

    The Kitchen Safety Bundle. Each day’s fridge and freezer record goes to %%CHECKLIST_APP%% after the day ends, and %%CHECKLIST_APP%% fills in its temperature-log check for you. No names are sent, only roles.

    Only an owner can connect %%CHECKLIST_APP%%.

    Your AI key for alert wording Owners only

    Each alert text and call has one line saying what to check first, worded by AI when it can and by a plain template when it cannot. Save your own Google AI Studio API key here and that line is written on your key, for your alerts only.

    Only an owner can save an AI key.
