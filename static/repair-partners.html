For refrigeration technicians

Repair leads from businesses whose fridge is failing.

%%APP_SHORT%% watches fridges and freezers in restaurants, shops, clinics and labs. When a unit looks like it is failing, or keeps getting warmer, we offer the business a repair quote. If they say yes, we send their details to one repair company that covers their area.

How a lead works

  • The business asks. Nothing is sent unless the business taps "Get a repair quote", reads what will be sent and confirms.
  • One company gets it. Each lead goes to one approved partner who covers that postcode, ZIP or city. When several cover the same area, we take turns, so leads are shared fairly.
  • What you get: the business name, the person to contact and the phone or email they chose, the address, and a plain summary of the problem: the kind of unit, what our monitoring saw, and since when.
  • What you never get: their readings, their sensors, or any access to their account.

What it costs

  • %%LEAD_FEE%% for each lead we send you. Nothing to join, nothing monthly.
  • If a lead is not a real job (a wrong number, nobody asked), mark it bad on your dashboard within %%BAD_DAYS%% days. We look at each one, and a lead we agree was bad is not charged.
  • We send you a statement and you pay us by transfer. Nothing is taken from you automatically.

We do not do repairs ourselves, and we do not vouch for any technician. We pass on the business's request; the business chooses whether to call you, and the work and the price are between you and them.
