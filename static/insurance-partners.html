For insurers and brokers

Fridges that are watched 24/7, and a report that shows it.

Spoiled stock is one of the claims you see most from restaurants, pharmacies and labs. %%APP_SHORT%% watches their fridges and freezers day and night, and texts and calls their staff until somebody answers.

What a business can show you

  • A monthly proof of monitoring report for each location: the sensors watched and how much of the time they were online, every temperature alert and how fast somebody acknowledged it, the longest gap in the data, and who gets alerted, by role.
  • A link you can check. Each report has a short code. Open the link and you see the same headline numbers. If the report was changed after it was made, the page says so.
  • The business decides. Only the business can make and share a report, and it can switch a link off at any time.

What you never see

Anything from inside a business's account: its readings, its staff, their phone numbers. The only thing you see is a report a business chooses to send you, and that names no people.

Refer businesses, earn a share

  • You get a partner code and a sign-up link.
